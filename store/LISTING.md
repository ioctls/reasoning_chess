**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Reasonable Chess is a chess trainer that explains the "why" behind every move, so you build intuition instead of memorizing openings.

It pairs two engines that run entirely on your phone:
• Stockfish, the world's strongest chess engine, works out what's true.
• Maia-3, a neural network trained on millions of human games, works out what's human: what players at your rating actually play.

Where the two disagree is where you learn.

LEARN MODE
• Instant feedback on every move, in plain language: forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, mate threats, loose pieces.
• "Hidden gem" or "common trap": see how often players at your level would find (or fall for) a move.
• Watch the better idea play out move by move, with the evaluation bar following along.
• Threat check: before you move, spot what your opponent's last move was really after.
• A 4-step hint ladder: forcing moves → which piece → which idea → the move.

PLAY MODE
• Play a human-like opponent at your chosen rating, with no help during the game.
• Then get a full report: your winning chances over the game, the key moments where it turned, and what to work on.

PRACTICE YOUR WEAK SPOTS
• Every mistake is tagged with what went wrong: left a piece hanging, missed their threat, missed a tactic, king safety…
• Drills bring your own mistakes back on a spaced schedule, grouped by issue.
• Style profile: see which rating you play like, and how your choices compare with the engine's.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works completely offline. The app has no internet permission at all.
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collection.
• Free and open source (AGPL-3.0).
```
